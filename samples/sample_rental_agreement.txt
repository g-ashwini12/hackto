RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT (SYNTHETIC SAMPLE - NOT A REAL CONTRACT)

This Rental Agreement is made on 1 March 2026 between Mr. Arjun Rao ("Landlord") and Ms. Priya Nair ("Tenant") for the flat at 12 Lake View Road, Dharwad.

1. Term. The tenancy begins on 1 April 2026 and runs for eleven (11) months, ending on 28 February 2027.

2. Rent. The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 15,000 on or before the 5th day of each month by bank transfer.

3. Late Payment. If rent is not received by the 10th day of the month, the Tenant shall pay a late fee of Rs. 500 for each week of delay.

4. Security Deposit. The Tenant has paid a refundable security deposit of Rs. 45,000. The Landlord shall return the deposit within 30 days after the Tenant vacates, less any amount for unpaid rent or damage beyond normal wear and tear.

5. Maintenance. The Tenant is responsible for minor repairs costing less than Rs. 1,000. The Landlord is responsible for structural repairs and plumbing.

6. Termination. Either party may terminate this agreement by giving two (2) months written notice. If the Tenant leaves before the end of the lock-in period of six (6) months, the Tenant shall forfeit one month of rent from the security deposit.

7. Subletting. The Tenant shall not sublet the premises without the prior written consent of the Landlord.

8. Use. The premises shall be used for residential purposes only.

9. Entry. The Landlord may enter the premises for inspection with reasonable notice.

10. Governing Terms. Any dispute shall be resolved as per applicable law.
